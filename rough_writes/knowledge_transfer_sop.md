# Knowledge Transfer (KT) SOP

## DevOps Department – FCI-CCM

## New Hire – First Week KT Plan

----

## Overview

Based on the information available on the FCI-CCM official website and related Devops hiring documentation,the DevOps role at FCI-CCM is focused on :
• CI/CD automation
• Cloud and infrastructure management
• Monitoring and logging
• Deployment reliability
• CCM platform support (especially VARTA)
• Security and operational stability
• Cross-functional coordination with QA, Infra, and Development teams

The products of the company revolve around enterprise banking and customer communication platforms like VARTA. This ensures that DevOps onboarding should emphasise reliability, compliance,uptime, automation, and monitoring rather than quick onboarding.

## KT Plan

----

## Objective

The purpose of this KT SOP is to integrate new DevOps team members into the FCI-CCM working environment, deployment tracks, monitoring practices, and infrastructure support processes.

This KT process ensures that new hirings gain an integral understanding  of the company's:

1. DevOps culture
2. CI/CD workflows
3. Infrastructure management and environment
4. Monitoring and incidence handling
5. Documentation and support processes

## Scope

This KT plan is applicable to all new onboarded engineers, associates, trainees, and support personnel at FCI-CCM who are assigned to the DevOps team.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### KT Lead

1. Conducts KT sessions for the new hires
2. Provides system and software access
3. Assigns shadowing activities
4. Validates onboarding completion

### Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)

1. Explains deployment and monitoring workflows
2. Exhibits troubleshooting and incident handling skills
3. Shares operational and infrastructure documentation

### New Hires

1. Attends training sessions
2. Reviews infrastructure SOPs and runbooks
3. Follows DevOps best practices and workflows
4. Participates in supervised tasks

-----

## Environment Overview

Newly onboarded DevOps team members should understand the following:

1. Development Environment at FCI-CCM
2. QA/UAT Environment
3. Staging Environment
4. Production Environment
5. Support Environment

All KT sessions should focus on explaining deployment flow, approvals, rollback process, and interaction between Development, QA, Security, Infrastructure, and Support teams.

-----

## First Week KT Plan

### Day 1 - Organisation & DevOps Introduction

1. Overview of the FCI-CCM business model and operations
2. Introduction to the VARTA platforms
3. Structure and responsibilities of the DevOps team
4. Escalation hierarchy and support flow
5. Uptime and operational expectations

### Day 2 - Systems and Infrastructure

1. Secure systems and VPN setup and access
2. Linux server setup and configuration
3. Internal Git repositories and environment
4. Docker/Kubernetes setup and configuration
5. Access management and permissions

### Day 3 - CI/CD and Deployment

1. CI/CD workflows and best practices with pipeline overview
2. Build and deployment workflows and lifecycle
3. Source control workflow
4. Rollback basics and workflow
5. Deployment tracking and monitoring

### Day 4 - Monitoring and Incident Handling

1. Monitoring and overview of dashboard
2. Alerts and severity levels and threats
3. Basics of log analysis
4. Incident handling workflow and escalation
5. Operational health checks

### Day 5 - Documentation and Shadowing

1. Documentation and SOPs
2. Ticket lifecycles using Jira and Service Desk
3. Daily operational checklists
4. Shadowing senior engineers
5. Participation in standups and supervised tasks

----

## Tools Exposure

New Hires should gain a foundational exposure to the following:

1. Git/Gitlab
2. Jenkins/ Gitlab CI
3. Docker
4. Kubernetes
5. Jira/ Service Desk
6. Linux Systems
7. Monitoring Dashboards
8. Logging Tools

## CI/CD Concepts

Newly onboarded DevOps hires are expected to understand the following:

1. Continuous Integration (CI)
2. Continuous Delivery/Deployment (CD)
3. Pipeline execution flow
4. Branching strategy and workflow
5. Deployment approvals
6. Rollback and release management

## Monitoring and Incident Handling

Newly onboarded DevOps hires are expected to understand the following:

1. Infrastructure monitoring
2. Application monitoring
3. Alerting and logging handling
4. Log review basics
5. Incident severity classification
6. Escalation procedures

## Documentation and Ticketing Standards

Documentation and ticketing standards are key to the DevOps culture at FCI-CCM. New hires should be briefed on the following:

1. SOP maintenance
2. Runbook usage
3. KT documentation
4. Ticket updating and management of lifecycles
5. Escalation documentation
6. Management of Jira and Service Desk tickets

## Shadowing and Supervised Tasks

New hires are expected to participate in the following:

1. Shadowing senior DevOps team members
2. Observe and understand deployment workflows
3. Review dashboards and logs
4. Attend daily standups and operational meetings
5. Perform supervised tasks and tasks assigned by the KT lead

Any independent changes to the production environment are strictly prohibited and should be reported immediately to the DevOps team.

## Security and Compliance

New hires are expected to understand and strictly adhere to the following:

1. Credentials should never be shared through unsecured channels
2. Least privilege principle must be followed
3. VPN and secure authentication are required and mandatory
4. Any access to production environment should be approved by the DevOps team
5. Compliance with FCI-CCM policies and regulations is mandatory

## Expected Outcomes after Week 1

At the end of the first week, new hires to the DevOps team should be able to:

1. Understand FCI-CCM DevOps culture and workflows
2. Understand deployment environments
3. Possess awareness of CI/CD workflows and best practices
4. Understand monitoring and logging processes
5. Navigate ticketing systems and documentation
6. Participate in supervised tasks and shadowing activities

## KT Completion Criteria

The KT process is considered complete when the following criteria are met:

1. All KT sessions are completed and attended by the new hires
2. Documentation is reviewed and approved by the KT lead
3. Access to production environment is approved by the DevOps team
4. All shadowing activities are completed
5. Team Lead approves the completion of onboarding process

-----

## Next Steps

This KT SOP is a living document and is subject to change as the DevOps team evolves. Any feedback or suggestions on how to improve the SOP are welcome and can be shared with the team leads.
